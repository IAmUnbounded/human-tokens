The interface is the ledger.

Human Tokens turns everyday computer use into a readable account of attention: what you wrote, what you read, what you watched, and where the day tilted.

What we are trying to make.

A small, honest instrumentation layer for humans: not surveillance, not productivity theater, just enough signal to understand whether a session created more than it consumed.

01 Tracker

Observe active work locally.

Track active apps, windows, idle time, categories, and keystroke-based output estimates.

02 Capture

Estimate incoming text and media.

Classify reading, AI, communication, video, social, and browser text into input tokens.

03 Dashboard

Show the ratio in real time.

Expose output, input, visible text, category mix, apps, sources, and daily timelines.

04 Reports

Keep a daily memory.

Generate concise reports that make attention patterns easier to review and improve.
